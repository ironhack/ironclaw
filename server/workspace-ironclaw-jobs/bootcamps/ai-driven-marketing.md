# AI-Driven Marketing Bootcamp

## Course Info
- **Duration:** 360 hours + 40h prework, 9 weeks full-time
- **Level:** Beginner to Junior
- **DB slug:** `ai-driven-marketing`

## Stack & Technologies
Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Google Tag Manager (GTM), Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads, HubSpot (CRM + email marketing), SEMrush, Google Search Console, Canva, ChatGPT Plus, Looker Data Studio, Make (Integromat, automation), Firebase, Campaign Manager, SimilarWeb, Hotjar

## Key Skills
- Digital marketing strategy (inbound + outbound, full-funnel)
- Buyer persona development and customer journey mapping
- SEO: on-page, off-page, technical SEO, GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
- Content marketing with AI (ChatGPT for copy, Canva for design)
- Email marketing and CRM (HubSpot segmentation, A/B testing)
- Paid advertising: Google Ads (Search, PMax), Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, programmatic
- Analytics and tracking: GA4 setup, GTM tags, attribution models
- Marketing automation (Make + HubSpot + ChatGPT integrations)
- Dashboard and reporting (Looker Data Studio)
- A/B testing methodology

## Language Note
International brands, e-commerce companies, and SaaS businesses with German offices (Amazon, Booking.com, HubSpot) run English-first marketing teams. SEO and copy roles targeting German consumers may require B1-B2 German. Analytics, paid media, and automation roles are largely language-agnostic. Look for roles at international companies or those with explicit "English working language" statements.
