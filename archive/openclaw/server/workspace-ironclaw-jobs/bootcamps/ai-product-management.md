# AI Product Management Bootcamp

## Course Info
- **Duration:** 360 hours, 9 weeks full-time (no separate prework listed)
- **Level:** Beginner to Junior
- **DB slug:** `ai-product-management`

## Stack & Technologies
Jira, Confluence, Figma, Lovable/Bubble/Glide (no-code MVP tools), ChatGPT/Custom GPTs, OpenAI API, GA4 (Google Analytics 4), Mixpanel, Notion, Miro, Canva

## Key Skills
- Product Management frameworks (Agile, Scrum, Kanban)
- Writing PRDs, roadmaps, and backlogs (AI-assisted)
- User research: personas, Jobs-To-Be-Done, user interviews
- Prototyping with Figma and no-code tools (Lovable, Bubble)
- Custom GPT integration into product prototypes
- Analytics dashboards with GA4 and Mixpanel
- Go-to-market strategy (market research, competitor tracking, pricing)
- A/B testing and product monitoring
- Stakeholder communication and presentation
- Responsible AI principles and ethics

## Language Note
International product teams at tech companies and startups default to English. Scrum/Agile PM roles at global companies (Zalando, Delivery Hero, Booking.com DE offices) rarely require German beyond B1. The Berlin PM and startup scene is especially English-first. Established German enterprises (SAP, Deutsche Telekom) may require stronger German for stakeholder-facing roles.
